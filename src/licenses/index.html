pfpg itself is distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. This page reproduces the license texts of the third-party software this site ships whose licenses require the text to travel with the software: the WebAssembly rasterizer with the renderer compiled into it, and the image encoders with the codecs compiled into their modules. Every library the site uses is credited in the About section of the main page, including the Rust libraries that resvg depends on.

  • svg2ui8a-MIT.txt svg2ui8a — the SVG rasterizer's JavaScript, bundled into the page.
  • resvg-LICENSE-MIT.txt / resvg-LICENSE-APACHE.txt resvg — the Rust SVG rendering library compiled into the rasterizer's WebAssembly module. Copyright the Resvg Authors. Used under the Apache-2.0 or MIT license; both texts are included, and you may choose either.
  • jsquash-webp-Apache-2.0.txt @jsquash/webp — the WebP encoder's JavaScript, bundled into the page.
  • jsquash-webp-codec-libwebp-BSD-3-Clause.md libwebp — the WebP codec, compiled into the WebP encoder's WebAssembly module.
  • jsquash-oxipng-Apache-2.0.txt @jsquash/oxipng — the PNG optimizer's JavaScript, bundled into the page.
  • jsquash-oxipng-codec-oxipng-MIT.md oxipng — the PNG optimizer, compiled into the PNG optimizer's WebAssembly module.
